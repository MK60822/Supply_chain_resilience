# Supply Chain Management — Generated Multi-Agent System

> Auto-generated by AgentForge from a natural-language description.

## Goal
To predict and mitigate supply chain disruptions before they impact business operations

## Agents
- **Real-Time Disruption Detector** (`disruption_monitor`): Monitors real-time data sources to identify potential supply chain disruptions.
- **Supply Chain Impact Assessor** (`impact_analyser`): Analyses the impact of identified disruptions on the organisation's supply chain operations.
- **Supplier Operational Risk Evaluator** (`supplier_risk_evaluator`): Evaluates the operational risk of affected suppliers based on their historical performance data.
- **Shortage and Delay Predictor** (`shortage_predictor`): Predicts potential shortages and estimates delivery delays resulting from supply chain disruptions.
- **Mitigation Strategy Recommender** (`mitigation_recommender`): Recommends effective mitigation strategies to minimise the impact of supply chain disruptions on business operations.

## Workflow
- user → disruption_monitor (always)
- disruption_monitor → impact_analyser (disruption detected)
- impact_analyser → supplier_risk_evaluator (impact assessed)
- supplier_risk_evaluator → shortage_predictor (risk evaluated)
- shortage_predictor → mitigation_recommender (shortage predicted)
- mitigation_recommender → user (always)

### Cloud Deployment (Fly.io)

This project contains a pre-configured `Dockerfile` and `fly.toml` for one-click cloud hosting:

1. Install Fly CLI: `curl -L https://fly.io/install.sh | sh`
2. Authenticate: `fly auth login`
3. Launch: `fly launch`
4. Deploy: `fly deploy --env GROQ_API_KEY=your_key GEMINI_API_KEY=your_key`
